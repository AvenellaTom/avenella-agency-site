The real cost of DIY

Wix looks cheap.
Until it doesn't

The subscription fee is the smallest number on the bill. Here is what the research actually says.

01

Your time has a price tag

Owners managing their own site spend 8 to 12 hours a month on updates, backups and fixes. At even £30 an hour that is £240 to £360 a month in lost productivity. More than our top plan.

02

Website downtime costs thousands

Emergency malware removal runs £75 to £150 an hour in the UK. Full recovery from data loss without a backup? £500 to £5,000, plus weeks offline and months of search ranking damage.

03

Cheap builds need rebuilding

Template sites carry technical debt from day one. Most cheap builds need a full rebuild within 12 to 18 months. The free site ends up the most expensive one you ever own.

Wix and Squarespace look like £13 to £22 a month. Add a domain and business email and the true annual cost hits £240 to £360 before you have touched the design. And you have still done all the work yourself.
